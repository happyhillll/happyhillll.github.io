Welcome to my page! I'm a PhD student in the Interdisciplinary Program in Cognitive Science at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Eunsoo Lee at the Meta Humanities Lab. I study how digital technology can preserve and reinterpret cultural records, and how people and AI interact with them.

My research interests include:

  • Digital Humanities — archives, metadata, and datasets for cultural heritage (especially performance ephemera),
  • Human-Computer Interaction and social reading tools,
  • Cultural Studies, AI Safety, and the relationship between humans and AI.

Before graduate school, I worked as a research assistant on the Human Rights and Tech Ethics Team at Kakao, and as an intern on the NLP Data Construction Team at NCSOFT. I received my M.A. in Cognitive Science from Seoul National University and my B.A. in East African Studies (minor in Statistics) from Hankuk University of Foreign Studies.

I also run KoVox, a living archive of Korean classical vocal recitals.

You can reach me at happyhill@snu.ac.kr.
