Minji Kim

PhD Student, Cognitive Science

I am a PhD student in the Interdisciplinary Program in Cognitive Science at Seoul National University, working at the Meta Humanities Lab with Prof. Eunsoo Lee. My research spans digital archives for cultural records (KoVox, KoVox Dataset), aesthetic and social reading (POCO, Semicolon), and the relationship between humans and AI, including AI ethics and safety.
